# Gate A pre-registration (fixed before any at-scale profiling run)

## Measurement
Profile the packages listed in `docs/prereg/2026-09-16-gate-a-packages.txt` (the servers needed for the Phase D evaluation set, drawn from the configuration corpus by how often each is configured). For each package record, from `profiles/stats.jsonl`: `tools`, `claims`, `verified`, `demoted`, `cleared_verified`, `cleared_unverified`, `missing_tools`, `unknown_tools`, wall-clock time and API cost.

Rows are deduplicated by (package, version), keeping the last row, because a rerun appends rather than replaces.

## Definitions
Every advertised tool falls into exactly one bucket: the model claimed labels for it (`claims`, split into `verified` and `demoted` by whether the deterministic checker confirmed its cited evidence), the model cleared it with no labels (`cleared_verified` or `cleared_unverified` by the same check), or the model did not judge it at all (`missing_tools`). Names the model judged that do not exist in the advertised surface are counted separately as `unknown_tools`.

- verified-claim rate = verified / claims, pooled over all packages.
- unverified-clear rate = cleared_unverified / tools, pooled.
- unjudged-tool rate = len(missing_tools) / tools, pooled.
- hallucinated-name rate = len(unknown_tools) / tools, pooled.

A package is *usable* when its verified-claim rate is at least 0.70, its unjudged-tool rate is at most 0.05, and its unverified-clear rate is at most 0.20.

`verified / claims` alone is not sufficient to pass this gate: a package can show a healthy claim rate while most of its tools were cleared without checkable evidence, which is why the clear rate is a criterion in its own right.

## Criteria (fixed now)
- A1 continue as planned: pooled verified-claim rate at least 0.80, pooled unverified-clear rate at most 0.20, and at least 80% of packages usable.
- A2 narrow: pooled verified-claim rate in [0.60, 0.80), or pooled unverified-clear rate in (0.20, 0.40], or 50-80% of packages usable. Restrict Phases B through E to the usable packages, shrink the evaluation set accordingly, and report the restriction as a threat to validity.
- A3 drop the technique: pooled verified-claim rate below 0.60, or pooled unverified-clear rate above 0.40, or fewer than half the packages usable, or a measured cost above USD 300 for the listed packages. The paper becomes measurement-only (claims C1 and C2) and Phases C through E are cancelled.

A hallucinated-name rate above 0.02 is not itself a gate criterion but must be reported, and if it occurs the adjudication prompt is revised and the affected packages re-profiled before the gate is read.

## Recording
Write `docs/results/<date>-gate-a.md` with the numbers, the branch taken, the measured cost, and the rewritten plan for the next phase. Do not carry the Phase A plan forward unchanged if the branch is A2 or A3.
